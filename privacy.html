Privacy Policy for Capy Tab Manager

Effective Date: May 20, 2026

We respect and value your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how Capy Tab Manager ("the Extension") handles and protects your information.

Our core philosophy is simple: What happens on your computer stays on your computer.

1. No Personal Data Collection

Capy Tab Manager does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to use the Extension.

2. Permissions and How We Use Them

The Extension requires specific browser permissions to provide its core dashboard and productivity features. All data processed by these permissions is kept strictly within your local browser instance:

  • Tabs & tabGroups: Used exclusively to display your open tabs on your custom dashboard, read tab titles/favicons, calculate local tab health, and allow you to group or close tabs easily.
  • Storage: Used solely to save your local to-do list decision matrix, saved tab lists, and custom dashboard configurations across browser sessions.
  • Geolocation: Used strictly on the client side to fetch local weather conditions for your new tab dashboard. This location data is never sent to us or saved.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics trackers, background advertising scripts, or external databases. If the Extension contacts an external API (such as a public weather API to load the dashboard weather feature), your location data is processed securely over HTTPS and is not retained.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because we do not collect or store any of your data, we have no data to sell, rent, or share with third parties, advertising platforms, or data brokers.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Your continued use of the Extension signifies your consent to the terms outlined here.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email or through our official support channels.
